# Where this rewrite has got to

Three briefs are in flight together. This is the running order and what is
actually done, so nothing gets lost between them.

## Done and proven

**The twenty-round economy** — `tools/test-economy.mjs`, 73 checks, both engines.

- 20 rounds, checkpoint at 15, alliances close after 10, final phase 16–20
- ₹0 starting balance; ₹5 Cr credited at the start of every round
- cash carries forward and is never wiped; ₹100 Cr a campaign, ₹400 Cr across four
- the round allowance is income, **not** a spending cap — a saved balance can
  go out in one round through bulk allocation
- a single move is still capped at a multiple of its own cost, so spending big
  needs breadth rather than depth. That is the anti-dumping property the
  earlier balance work established and it is deliberately kept
- income is keyed by round number, so a refresh, reconnection or repeated
  advance cannot pay anybody twice
- a full ledger behind every figure

**Territory** — `js/data/regions.js`, generated by `tools/build-regions.mjs`.

- Punjab's three real regions: Majha 25 seats, Doaba 23, Malwa 69
- 23 real districts. The brief asked for up to 70 priority districts; Punjab
  has 23 and inventing 47 more was not an option, so the ceiling is 23
- holding **every** seat in a district pays its grant each round, into a purse
  locked to that district's region
- grants scale with district size, ₹2 Cr (2 seats) to ₹30 Cr (14 seats)
- **a grant is for a district taken, not one inherited.** The opening board is
  dealt from the sitting MLAs and hands one party six districts on average —
  eighteen on some seeds, worth ₹136 Cr a round. Paying for those would settle
  the election on the deal, so opening districts pay nothing until they are
  lost and won back

**Server-side round machinery**

- `roundReady` per player, `Rounds::awaiting()`, `everyoneReady()`, `readyCount()`
- a round closes when the clock expires **or** everybody still in has pressed
  END ROUND
- `endround` and `priority` API routes
- host picks 2 / 3 / 5 minute rounds at start, validated against the config
- `Alliances.php`: offers, accept/decline, the round-10 lock, and the round-15
  checkpoint review (which never eliminates from a field of two, and never
  eliminates a campaign still within reach)

## Things worth remembering

- PHP here uses **no namespace**. Two new classes were written with
  `namespace CMP;` and would have been a runtime fatal; both are fixed.
- `play()` has a local `record` variable, so the ledger writer is `ledger()`.
- Hostinger's CDN answers rapid asset requests with a 403 challenge page that
  looks exactly like a broken deploy. Space out live checks.
