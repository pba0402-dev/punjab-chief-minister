## Description

> **Fight a Punjab Assembly election, seat by seat.**
>
> Found your own party — name it, badge it, pick its symbol and its colour —
> and contest all 117 constituencies across twenty rounds.
>
> **The map is the game.** Tap a seat and decide what to put into it. Getting
> into somewhere nobody holds costs a crore at most, so nobody buys their way
> in ahead of anyone else. After that, spend what you like.
>
> **Win a district and it pays you.** Lead every constituency in a district and
> its grant arrives every round — but the money is locked to the region that
> earned it, so where you fight decides what you can afford next.
>
> **Take a seat outright and it is yours.** No one can campaign there again,
> including you.
>
> **Borrow, if you dare.** The bank takes its interest the moment it lends, and
> the bill falls due four rounds later whether you have it or not.
>
> **Play with three friends** on a shared code, or on your own against three
> opponents that read the map and spend against you.
>
> Election night is read out region by region — Malwa, Majha, Doaba — district
> by district, before the overall standing.
>
> ---
>
> Election Time is a strategy game. The constituency names, numbers and
> districts are real public information; every party, candidate, percentage and
> result in it is invented by the game and its players. It is not a poll, not a
> prediction, and not connected to any government, election commission,
> political party or candidate.

## Review notes

> Election Time is a fictional turn-based strategy game. There is no account,
> no login and no in-app purchase — tap "Election Time" on the opening screen
> to play immediately against three computer opponents.
>
> To test multiplayer, tap "Play / Join Election" → "Create game" to get a
> five-letter code, then join it from a second device.
>
> The game uses the real names and numbers of the 117 Punjab Assembly
> constituencies, which are public information. Every party, candidate,
> percentage and result is invented by the game. It is not a poll or a
> prediction and is not affiliated with any government, election commission,
> political party or candidate.
>
> Profile deletion: My Profile → Delete my profile. It removes the profile from
> the server immediately.

**No test account is needed** — there is nothing to sign in to. Say so; a blank
credentials field with no explanation causes a rejection.
