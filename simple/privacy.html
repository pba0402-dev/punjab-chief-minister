Privacy

Election Time · Punjab Assembly

There are no accounts in this game. No email address, no password, no phone number, no social login. What it keeps is a name you chose, a face you picked from a fixed set, and what happened in the elections you played.

What is kept in your browser

Four things, in your browser's own storage. Clearing your site data removes all of them, and none of them is a cookie — this game sets no cookies.

  • Your profile — the name you typed, the face you chose, and a random id
  • Your saved game — so closing a tab does not lose an election
  • Your session — a game code and token, so you can rejoin a multiplayer game
  • Your settings — whether music and sound are on, and how loud

What is kept on the server

Your profile. The name you chose, the face you chose, and your record: elections played and won, seats, districts, grants and achievements. It is filed under a random 16-character id that your browser generated the first time you played. Nothing links that id to you as a person.

The elections you played. The board, the rounds, the parties everyone invented, the alliances and the result. A multiplayer election is a shared record between the people who played it.

Counts. How many people have played, how many elections have finished, how many rounds have been played. These are totals with no identifiers in them.

How visits are counted

To tell one person refreshing five times from five people arriving, the server turns your address and browser description into a short hash. The recipe includes the day and a secret, and the address itself is never stored — so the hash cannot be matched against tomorrow's, and cannot be matched against anything outside this game.

It can answer how many people came. It cannot answer did this person come back. Daily figures are deleted after 90 days; only the totals survive.

What is never collected

No location. No contacts. No camera, microphone or photo library — the face on your profile is one of a fixed set of pictures that ship with the game, not something you upload. No advertising identifier. No device identifier. No crash reporting. No payment details. No chat.

Who else sees it

Nobody. There is no analytics service, no advertising network, no third-party software development kit, and no data broker. This game has no third-party runtime dependencies at all: it talks to its own server and to nothing else.

Other players in your election see what an election makes public — your party, your name, your seats and where you lead. They never see your cash, your private moves or anything about your device.

Deleting your profile

Open My Profile and choose Delete my profile. It removes your profile from the server and clears everything this browser kept. It cannot be undone.

Elections you played stay as they are, because other people played them too and removing your part would rewrite their game.

Children

This game is not directed at children and asks for no information that would identify one.
