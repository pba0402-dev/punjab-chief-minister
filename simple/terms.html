Terms of use

Election Time · Punjab Assembly

Election Time is a strategy game. It is not a poll, a prediction, or a record of any real election, and it is not connected to any government, election commission, political party or candidate.

What the game is

You found an invented party and contest a fictional twenty-round election across the 117 Punjab Assembly constituencies. The constituency names, numbers and districts are real public information. Everything else — the parties, the candidates, every percentage and every seat — is produced by the game from what players did in it.

Nothing in this game describes how to do anything in a real election. The corruption and negative-campaign actions are weighted dice rolls with in-game consequences; they are not instructions and do not represent any real method.

Playing

You may play for your own enjoyment. Please do not attempt to break the game for other people: interfering with the server, automating play to distort the statistics, or abusing the multiplayer system spoils an election that other people are in the middle of.

Your profile

There is no account and no password. Your profile lives in your browser and on the game's server under a random id. If you lose that browser's storage, you lose the profile — there is no recovery, because there is nothing to recover it with.

You may delete your profile at any time from the My Profile screen.

What you type

You choose your display name, your party's name, its abbreviation and its slogan. In a multiplayer game the other players see them.

Do not use a name that is abusive, that impersonates a real person or organisation, or that would be illegal to publish. Names that do may be removed and the profile behind them may be deleted.

To report a name or anything else you have seen in a game, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the game code.

Faces and symbols

The candidate pictures and party symbols in this game are chosen from a fixed set that ships with it. You cannot upload your own.

Multiplayer

A multiplayer election is a shared record between the people playing it. If you leave, the election carries on without you and you can rejoin while it is still running. If you end a game deliberately, that is final.
