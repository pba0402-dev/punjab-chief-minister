# Content rights review

Every piece of artwork and named content in the game, and whether its rights
are clear. Nothing here is asserted to be cleared unless it demonstrably is.

## ⚠️ Needs your rights review before submission

### Candidate portraits — `simple/assets/portraits/`

Ten PNG files, supplied by you, named after and depicting **real, living
Indian political figures**: Arvind Kejriwal, Bhagwant Mann, Captain Amarinder
Singh, Charanjit Singh Channi, Narendra Modi, Navjot Sidhu, Parkash Badal,
Partap Singh Bajwa, Raja Warring, Sukhbir Badal.

They are illustrations rather than photographs, but they are recognisable
likenesses of identifiable people.

**Why this needs attention:**

- **Ownership.** Who drew them, and do you hold the rights? If they were
  generated or commissioned, the licence terms matter.
- **Personality rights.** India recognises personality rights, and several of
  these individuals have asserted them. Depicting a living politician in a
  commercial game, winning and losing simulated elections, is a use they have
  not consented to.
- **App Store guideline 5.2.** Apple rejects apps using a real person's name or
  likeness without permission.

**This is not a technical problem and I cannot resolve it in code.** The game
was originally built with twenty-four invented, drawn characters for exactly
this reason; those were replaced at your instruction. Reverting to invented
characters removes the issue entirely.

### Party symbols — `simple/assets/party-symbols/`

Six PNG files depicting the symbols of **real, registered Indian political
parties**: AAP (broom), BJP, INC, SAD, LOK, JSP — several captioned with the
party's full name.

**Why this needs attention:**

- Registered election symbols in India are allotted by the Election Commission
  and associated with specific parties.
- Party names and logos are protected marks.
- The same guideline 5.2 applies.

The game originally shipped sixteen invented symbols — a tree, a lamp, a river
— drawn as geometry, for the same reason.

## What the game says about itself

The README and the in-game "About the map" note state that constituency names
and districts are real public information and that everything else — parties,
candidates, percentages, results — is invented. That framing is accurate for
the game's *data*. It is not accurate for the *portraits and symbols now
installed*, and either the assets or the framing needs to change.

## Recommendation

Replace the portraits and party symbols with invented ones before submitting.
It is a mapping change, not a code change: `simple/js/data/asset-map.js` points
each id at a filename, so new artwork under the same ids works immediately.

If you intend to keep them, get written permission or legal advice first. Do
not submit on the assumption that "it's a game" or "they're illustrations, not
photos" will carry it.
